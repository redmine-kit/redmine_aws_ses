# Project email settings

The project settings tab **Email** sets how the project receives email: whether it receives email, its address, and the values of the issues created from email. It is shown to members with the permission **Manage email settings**. Only administrators can choose **Create a user** for **Unknown senders**, and change the forwarding settings: **Forwarders** (addresses or domains whose forwarded email is fetched even when it fails DMARC), **Forwarded email** (fetch a member's forward as its original, with the member as a watcher and a note by the member) and **Author of forwarded email** (which original senders may be the author); members see them disabled.

More: [Redmine AWS SES: project email settings](https://redmine-kit.github.io/redmine_aws_ses/admin.html#project-email-settings).
