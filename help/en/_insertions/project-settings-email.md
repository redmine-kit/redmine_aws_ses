## Email

With [Redmine Kit AWS SES](../aws-ses/project-mail.md) and its per-project addresses, the **Email** tab sets how the project receives email: whether email to its address is fetched (**Receive email**), the values of the issues created from email, what becomes of email from **Unknown senders**, and, for administrators, the forwarding settings. Members need the permission **Manage email settings**.
