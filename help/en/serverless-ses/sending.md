# Sending email

Administration › Serverless › **Sending email** shows the state of Redmine's email on Amazon SES: the **SES account** (production access, whether sending is enabled, the quota of the last 24 hours and the rate), the **Domain** and whether it is verified for sending, and the **Suppression list**, the addresses SES does not send to.

When a mailbox works again, an administrator removes its address from the suppression list on this page.

More: [Redmine Serverless SES: administration pages](https://redmine-kit.github.io/redmine_serverless_ses/admin.html).
